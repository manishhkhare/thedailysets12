HAIR NUTRITION GUIDE

Let your hair reflect your health.

Beautiful, strong-looking hair does not start with expensive products alone. A balanced diet and adequate nutrient intake are important parts of overall health.

Below are vegetarian foods that provide useful nutrients and can be included in a varied, balanced diet.

01 — 10

Vegetarian Superfoods

Simple foods. Useful nutrients. Easy ways to add them to your everyday meals.

01
🌱

Chia Seeds

Nutrients

Omega-3, Protein, Zinc

How to use

Soak 1 tbsp overnight and add to breakfast or smoothies.

🌾

Flaxseeds

Omega-3 and Vitamin E.

How to use

Grind and add to oats, smoothies or knead into roti dough.

🥜

Walnuts

Omega-3 and biotin.

A convenient plant-based source of healthy fats. Try 4–5 soaked walnuts in the morning if they suit your diet.

🎃

Pumpkin Seeds

Zinc, Magnesium and Iron.

Eat roasted or sprinkle over salads, oats or cereals.

🌿

Moringa Powder

Iron, Vitamin A and Zinc.

Can be mixed in water, smoothies or suitable foods. Start with a small amount if new to it.

🫘

Chickpeas (Chana)

Protein, Zinc and Biotin.

Eat boiled in salads, use as a snack, or prepare hummus.

🧀

Paneer / Tofu

Protein, Calcium and Iron.

Use around 100–150g as part of lunch or dinner, depending on your overall dietary needs.

🥜

Peanuts (Mungfali)

Biotin and Protein.

Enjoy roasted peanuts as a snack or use natural peanut butter with breakfast.

🍋

Amla

Vitamin C and antioxidants.

Eat fresh/raw or use a suitable amount of amla powder mixed with water or food.

🌿

Fenugreek Seeds (Methi Dana)

Protein, Iron, Nicotinic Acid and Lecithin.

✓ Can be included as part of a balanced diet

✓ Soak overnight and consume in the morning

✓ Or roast, grind and mix with suitable foods

✓ Can also be sprouted and eaten with meals

DAILY BASICS

Don't forget the basics.

💧

Hydration

Drink enough water throughout the day. Individual needs vary based on body size, activity, climate and other factors.

Stay consistently hydrated
☀️

Vitamin D

Vitamin D is difficult to obtain from food alone. Sunlight and, when appropriate, testing or supplements can be discussed with a healthcare professional.

Check your levels if needed
